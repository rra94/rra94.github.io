Flowers of Spring

05 Sep 2026

The wildflowers don’t care
Step on them if you must
Mangle them with heavy boots
Blue and red blend to purple
Then to brown they go back
Till again comes spring

But Dandelions wait
To be plucked by you
Both yearning to go places
Filled with orange skies
Dancing in the gentle wind
Giving wishes wings
